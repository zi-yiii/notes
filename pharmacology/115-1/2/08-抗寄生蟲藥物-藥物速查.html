Antiparasitic agents · Drug reference

MechanismsBlock 2

Benzimidazoles

Mebendazole; albendazole

→ β-tubulin binding → microtubule assembly ↓

Main uses · 用途

Intestinal nematodes; albendazole also selected tissue cestode infections including hydatid disease / neurocysticercosis.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Short courses: GI effects; prolonged albendazole: hepatic and marrow toxicity.

Distinguish · 易混點

Teacher priority: microtubule mechanism. Neurocysticercosis treatment depends on lesion status; dying parasites can worsen inflammation.

Depolarizing anthelmintics

Pyrantel pamoate

→ Nicotinic receptor activation → spastic paralysis

Main uses · 用途

Selected intestinal roundworms including pinworm / Ascaris / hookworm.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

GI cramps; headache / dizziness.

Distinguish · 易混點

Poor absorption helps intestinal action; not a universal treatment for tissue parasites.

Ca²⁺-permeability / tegument agents

Praziquantel

→ Ca²⁺ influx and tegument disruption

Main uses · 用途

Many cestodes and trematodes; choice depends on species.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

GI effects, dizziness; parasite-death inflammatory reactions.

Distinguish · 易混點

Teacher priority: two mechanisms. Not all flukes are covered: Fasciola is a key exception.

Luminal cestocides

Niclosamide

→ Oxidative phosphorylation impaired

Main uses · 用途

Intestinal adult tapeworm infection.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

GI upset.

Distinguish · 易混點

Poor absorption: no treatment of tissue cysticercosis / hydatid disease. Do not equate ova, luminal adults, and tissue cysts.

Host-assisted antifilarials

Diethylcarbamazine (DEC)

→ Changes parasite / host interactions → clearance ↑

Main uses · 用途

Selected lymphatic filariasis; specialist / species-based use.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Fever, rash, headache; inflammatory reactions to dying parasites.

Distinguish · 易混點

Not a universal choice for all filarial infections; onchocerciasis / heavy Loa loa burden need particular caution.

4-aminoquinolines

Chloroquine; amodiaquine

→ Heme detoxification impaired

Main uses · 用途

Blood-stage malaria when parasite susceptibility / local regimen permits.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Chloroquine: retinal toxicity with prolonged exposure; rash, headache, QT effects. Amodiaquine has distinct hepatic / hematologic risks.

Distinguish · 易混點

Not universal first line in all regions. No hypnozoite eradication; corneal deposits and retinal injury are not identical.

Other quinoline blood-stage agents

Quinine; quinidine; mefloquine

→ Blood-stage antimalarial effects; molecular details differ

Main uses · 用途

Drug- and resistance-specific malaria contexts; not a universal regimen list.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Quinine: cinchonism, hypoglycemia, cardiac effects; quinidine: cardiac toxicity; mefloquine: major neuropsychiatric risk.

Distinguish · 易混點

Do not transfer all quinine risks / indications unchanged to mefloquine. No hypnozoite eradication.

Anti-relapse 8-aminoquinolines

Primaquine

→ Dormant liver-stage activity; oxidative injury is involved

Main uses · 用途

P. vivax / P. ovale anti-relapse treatment after appropriate blood-stage therapy; selected transmission / prevention uses.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Hemolysis with G6PD deficiency; methemoglobinemia.

Distinguish · 易混點

Quantitative G6PD testing before use; pregnancy contraindication. Not adequate treatment of acute blood-stage illness alone.

Mitochondrial / antifolate combination

Atovaquone + proguanil

→ bc₁ inhibition plus proguanil potentiation; cycloguanil inhibits DHFR

Main uses · 用途

Malaria prevention / treatment in applicable settings.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

GI effects, headache; renal / pregnancy restrictions depend on indication.

Distinguish · 易混點

Active against developing liver stages does NOT mean kills dormant hypnozoites; food improves atovaquone absorption.

Artemisinin derivatives

Artemisinin; artesunate; artemether; dihydroartemisinin

→ Endoperoxide activation → reactive intermediates / parasite damage

Main uses · 用途

Rapid blood-stage parasite clearance; ACT for applicable uncomplicated malaria, IV artesunate for severe malaria.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

GI effects; delayed hemolysis can follow IV artesunate.

Distinguish · 易混點

No hypnozoite eradication; avoid oral artemisinin monotherapy. Do not copy the lecture as a blanket pregnancy ban.

Tissue-active nitroimidazoles

Metronidazole

→ Reduced reactive intermediates damage DNA

Main uses · 用途

Invasive intestinal / extraintestinal amebiasis; then luminal therapy. Also other organism-specific uses.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

GI upset, metallic taste; neuropathy / neurologic toxicity with exposure.

Distinguish · 易混點

Controls invasive trophozoites but is insufficient to reliably eradicate luminal carriage; not simply a direct RNA-polymerase inhibitor.

Luminal amebicides

Diloxanide furoate; iodoquinol

→ Luminal activity; precise mechanism not established

Main uses · 用途

Asymptomatic E. histolytica carriage and luminal eradication after tissue treatment; availability differs.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Diloxanide: flatulence / GI effects. Iodoquinol: GI effects; optic / peripheral neurotoxicity with excess or prolonged use.

Distinguish · 易混點

Not adequate monotherapy for invasive disease. Cysts are in intestinal lumen / stool, not a dormant cyst reservoir embedded in the wall.

Historical tissue agents

Emetine; dehydroemetine

→ Amoebic protein synthesis inhibition

Main uses · 用途

Historical second-line tissue amebiasis context.

Adverse effects / interactions · 副作用

Cardiotoxicity / arrhythmia, GI and neuromuscular toxicity.

Distinguish · 易混點

Not a routine mandatory add-on for every liver abscess; current management generally starts with nitroimidazole plus luminal therapy.
